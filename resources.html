Frequently asked questions

Helpful Information

How can I request a new internet connection?

Contact Vital Byte by phone or email. Explain your location, preferred package and the type of connection you need. The team can guide you about the next steps.

How do I choose an internet package?

Think about how many people and devices will use the connection, and whether you mainly browse, study, stream or work online. Compare the sample plans on the Internet Packages page.

How can I ask for technical support?

Contact the support team and describe the issue, when it started and any equipment indicators or error messages you can see.

Can I check my data usage online?

This project includes a sample dashboard to demonstrate how usage information could be displayed. Real-time usage checking requires a connected customer-management system.

What information should I prepare before contacting support?

Prepare your name, contact number, service location, account details if applicable and a short description of the issue.
